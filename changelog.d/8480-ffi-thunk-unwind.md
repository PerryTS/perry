fix(ffi): `bun:ffi`'s per-arity symbol thunks and `close_thunk` are `extern "C-unwind"` (#8479). They call `invoke_from_closure`, which throws the documented "symbol … was called after close()" error; under plain `extern "C"` that unwind hit Rust's abort-on-unwind shim inside the thunk, so a use-after-close aborted the process on Linux instead of throwing — `tier1_every_ffi_type_against_test_dylib` stayed red after #8464, which had converted only the closure-dispatch callers. A call through an `extern "C-unwind"` pointer does not remove the callee's own nounwind guard.
