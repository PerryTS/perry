- **`ServerResponse` listener table.** A `ServerResponse` starts with Node 26.12's preset `_events` (`close`, `error`, `finish`, `prefinish`, `drain`) and gets `_eventsCount` with its first listener. A removed listener leaves its key as `undefined`, and `eventNames()` skips such keys. The server registers its own `'finish'` listener on every response, as Node does, and `writableFinished` stays `false` until the socket has taken the last chunk.
