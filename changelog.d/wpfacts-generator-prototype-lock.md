- runtime/wpfacts: `%GeneratorPrototype%` and `%AsyncGeneratorPrototype%` join the locked built-in prototypes (DEC 250): replacing, deleting or redefining `next`, `return`, `throw` or `@@toStringTag`, or relinking the object, throws the locked-prototype TypeError; `constructor` and new members stay writable, and a generator function's own `prototype` stays the program's. The whole-program solver treats those members as final on a generator object, so a value a generator yields stays known after a store into an unknown value. A generator object now inherits from whatever its function's `prototype` holds (a replaced object, per closure context), not only the original object.
