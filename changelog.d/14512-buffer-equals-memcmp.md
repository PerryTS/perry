`buf.equals(other)` compiles to an inline length compare and one equality-only `memcmp` at sites the whole-program facts prove call `Buffer.prototype.equals`, when both operands are sealed constant-length byte views (a `Buffer` or `Uint8Array`), with no runtime test; every other site is the ordinary method call. Based on the fixed-size `Buffer.equals` intrinsic by jdalton (#14226), without the LLVM patch or the syntactic shadow scan.
