An HTTP/2 server session emits `'close'` from its socket's close callback, as Node does: `session.destroy()` now emits it, `session.close()` emits it once the socket has closed, and sessions destroyed in one turn close newest first, each followed by its ticks. `session.closed` stays `false` after `destroy()`, and `destroyed` stays `false` until a closing session's socket has closed.
