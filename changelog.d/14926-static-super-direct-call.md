`super.m()` in a static method calls the parent's declared static body directly when the compiler can name it (#12213). The site reuses the static-call memo over the home class's and the owner's function-object shapes, so replacing or deleting the parent's static, relinking the home's `[[Prototype]]`, and a `.call` receiver still take the live lookup. The static-call memo's inline hit now also applies on `arm64-apple-*` targets, where every guarded static call used to ask the runtime.
