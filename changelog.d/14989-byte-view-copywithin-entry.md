`Uint8Array`/`Buffer` `copyWithin` calls take a direct builtin-method entry (`js_bm_copyWithin1..3`) instead of the universal by-name dispatcher: a byte cell with no property bag, while `Buffer.prototype` is unwritten, runs the memmove after one header test. Small copies (emscripten's `_emscripten_memcpy_big` shape) are about 3x faster per call at 512 B. An explicit `undefined` `end` now copies to the end of the view (#14971).
