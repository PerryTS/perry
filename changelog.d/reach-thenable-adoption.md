Fix (whole-program facts, activation reach): a promise resolve function adopts a thenable. In the solve the resolve function the executor of `new Promise` receives (and the one a thenable's `then` receives) copied its argument as it is, so a thenable given to it never had its `then` called: that `then` was "inactive" while it ran (check mode `PERRY_VERIFY_FACTS=1`: `Activation / inactive body: body entered` on `promise/test_gap_promise_tick_parity` and `promise/test_new_promise_only_thenable`), and `PERRY_FACTS_DCE=1` trapped in the removed body. One rule now decides every resolution (`Cons::Fulfil`; the resolve function, `Promise.resolve`, a then-callback's result and `await` share it): a value fulfils the promise as itself only when its `then` read may hold something no call takes (a primitive, `undefined`, a non-function object, an unknown value); otherwise it is adopted (a native promise passes on its settled value, a thenable's `then` is called with fresh resolve functions and what they are given flows on). A reject function keeps the reason as it is. `PERRY_FACTS_REACH_THENABLE=0` restores the previous model.
