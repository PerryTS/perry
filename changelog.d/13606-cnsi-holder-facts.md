A read of a field that holds only instances of prefix-stable classes (or null/undefined) is now a proven receiver when the object it is read from is one: `this.current.run(n)`, `this.current.id` lower through the facts (a slot load, the class-id switch of direct calls) with no probe. The field facts carry the classes of every object any write stores there (`FactsField::classes`, `nullable`); a field that ever holds a thrown value, a function, a builtin, another primitive, interpreted values, a declared or reserved key gives no fact, and a nullable field keeps the receiver test. Per unit never proves more than the whole program.
