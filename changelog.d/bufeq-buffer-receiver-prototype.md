A Buffer or Uint8Array whose prototype was replaced with `Object.setPrototypeOf(buf, X)` or `buf.__proto__ = X` now uses that prototype like Node: method calls and member reads find `X`'s members (an override is called, a member `X` lacks reads `undefined` and calling it throws "is not a function", `null` or `Uint8Array.prototype` hide the Buffer methods), while every other Buffer keeps the built-in fast path; `buf.__proto__` now reads the real prototype instead of `undefined`.
