Fix a SIGSEGV in `Map.prototype.set` after a copying minor that promoted the young generation in place without tracing it (#12141). That cycle marks nothing, but the from-space Map walk read every unmarked Map as dead and freed the storage of live ones, including a rooted Map. Every copying-minor side-allocation finalizer and prune (Map, Set, Error diagnostics, lazy JSON tapes, promise contexts and the address-keyed side tables) now asks one predicate, `gc::minor_side_owner_is_dead`. It counts an owner as dead only when the owner is unmarked, unforwarded, unpinned and untenured, and sits in from-space. Retagged promoted blocks are no longer from-space, so their objects wait for the old generation.
