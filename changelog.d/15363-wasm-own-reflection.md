On `--target web`, `Reflect.getOwnPropertyDescriptor`, `Reflect.defineProperty`, `Reflect.has`, `Reflect.ownKeys`,
`Object.getOwnPropertyDescriptors`, `Object.hasOwn`, `Object.getOwnPropertyNames` and `Object.getOwnPropertySymbols`
answer as in Node, a computed object-literal key defines its property, a function declaration has its `name`, and
`JSON.stringify` omits functions.
