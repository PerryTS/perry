Fix (whole-program facts, activation reach): a callback a spread argument supplies to a built-in is no longer called inactive. A call site keeps its direct arguments and its spread arguments apart, and positions are not kept (`f(...a, b)` and `f(b, ...a)` are both `args [b]`, `spread [a]`). The hand-written built-in rules read their positions among the direct arguments only (`c.args.first()`, `c.args.get(1)`), so `xs.forEach(...[cb])` had no callback and `cb`'s body was "inactive" while it ran: check mode (`PERRY_VERIFY_FACTS=1`) reported `Activation / inactive body: body entered` (parity fixture `spread/test_gap_6718_spread_call_native_method`), and `PERRY_FACTS_DCE=1` trapped in the removed body. The same held for every position such a rule reads: a `thisArg`, the initial value of `reduce`, an element `splice` inserts, the mapper of `Array.from`, the reviver of `JSON.parse`, the handlers of `then` / `catch`, a listener given to `EventEmitter.on`. The rules now read their positions through one helper (`Model::positional`, the declared effects' rule: position `i` holds a direct argument at index `i` or before, or an element of a spread argument): the Array, iterator-helper, collection, promise, RegExp and EventEmitter methods, the `URL` constructor, and every namespace rule that reads `arg(i)`. A rule that reads "no argument there" (`reduce` without an initial value, a missing `thisArg`, `then` without a handler) keeps that case beside the spread. `PERRY_FACTS_REACH_SPREAD_ARGS=0` restores the previous model.
