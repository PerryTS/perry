macOS/iOS: GC arena blocks now come from their own 1 MiB anonymous mappings, tagged `VM_MEMORY_APPLICATION_SPECIFIC_2` (241), instead of from mimalloc. They show up apart from mimalloc (tag 240) in `vmmap` and footprint tools. Idle pool and Eden pages are released with `MADV_FREE_REUSABLE`, which drops them from the physical footprint at once, and are reactivated with `MADV_FREE_REUSE` on pool handoff and in `Arena::set_current`, so the bump path carries no check. Because block allocation no longer drives mimalloc's deferred purges, a fresh region map runs `mi_collect(true)` at most once per 10 ms per thread (disabled with the existing `PERRY_GC_MALLOC_PURGE=0`). Without it, an Effect workload peaked 5 MiB higher. On an M1 Max, retired instructions match mimalloc-backed blocks on every measured program. Peak footprint is lower on Fastify (−6.6 MiB), qs, Zod, commander and buffer workloads, and within noise elsewhere. Linux (R2 reservation) and other targets are unchanged. Also restores the build of a macOS-only `dynamic_object` test fixture whose `BufferHeader` fields had become private.
