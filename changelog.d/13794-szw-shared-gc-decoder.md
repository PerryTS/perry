Twelve cold and warm GC walkers (remembered-set rebuilds, dirty-card scans, the leaf service, image cells, old-evacuation rewrite, malloc-mark remembering, the promoted-cohort check) now share one out-of-line copy of the GC slot-layout decoder instead of each inlining its own, which makes every linked binary about 200 KB smaller. The minor-GC copying drain, its preflight and the full-mark trace keep their inlined decoder. Startup instruction counts are unchanged.
