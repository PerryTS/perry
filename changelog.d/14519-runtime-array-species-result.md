`Array.prototype.slice`, `splice`, `concat`, `map` and `filter` fill the result of a species constructor that is not `%Array%` with CreateDataPropertyOrThrow and, for `slice`, `splice` and `concat`, end with `Set(A, "length", n, true)`: an `Object` species result gets its `length`, a typed-array species throws a TypeError where its `length` cannot be set or an index is missing, and a species constructor returning a frozen, sealed, non-extensible, shorter or longer Array no longer has its slots written directly. A setter on an index of the result is replaced, not called, and holes stay absent in a `splice` result.
