`--target web`: a `perry/ui` app lays out as on macOS. `stackSetAlignment` takes NSLayoutAttribute values, `widgetSetHugging` honours its priority, `stackSetDistribution` and the Fill rule share a stack's space, stacks start from their content size, `App` fills the browser window and `textSetFontWeight` reads Apple's weight scale. `widgetSetOnClick`, `textSetNumberOfLines`, `getDeviceIdiom`, `shareUrl` and `notificationRegisterRemote` work on the web, and `ImageSymbol(name)` draws `symbols/<name>.svg` tinted by `imageSetTint` (#15452).
