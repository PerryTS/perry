The whole-program fact `family_reach` now removes the typed-array / Buffer runtime group from the link: when the text detector selects the group and the facts say the program never creates or observes a typed array, Buffer or ArrayBuffer, the generated installer does not name `js_runtime_install_typedarray` (about 120 KB less in such a binary: a class named `CommandBuffer` is enough for the detector) and the image claims the family with `js_runtime_families_never`; a byte cell born under the claim aborts with a message instead of meeting empty hooks. Everything else is the detector's answer, so a binary is the one built before or a smaller one. `PERRY_FACTS_FAMILY_LINK=0` restores the detector's decision with no claim; `PERRY_FACTS_FAMILY_LINK=full` makes the install follow the fact both ways (a program the detector misses links the group). The declared births the fact rests on are now checked complete by `perry-gc-effects births --strict`: a bound-method member of a native module is checked on its export body, a body the compiler merged on its function-info record, and the walk follows whoever takes a function's address (in code or through data), so a native that schedules a completion or registers a callback that later allocates a byte cell is checked itself. The family table gains the modules the check found (`readline`, `readline/promises`, `repl`, `sea`, `test`, `test/reporters`, `url`, `vm`) and `process.stdin`.
