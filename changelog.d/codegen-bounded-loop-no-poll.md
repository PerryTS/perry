A loop that is provably bounded and call-free takes no back-edge GC poll (`stmt::loops::bounded_poll`). Bounded: the loop has a counter whose whole-program interval is `[lo, hi]` and every iteration moves it at least once, always the same way, by a nonzero integer literal (a `for` update, the first or last statement of a `while` body, an operand of its test; a step `test ? a : b` of both signs counts when it is the only write and `test` cannot change in the loop), so it runs at most `hi - lo + 1` iterations per entry. Call-free: no block emitted for the loop (tests, body, update, nested loops, cold arms, every clone) holds a call that may collect or run user code; LLVM intrinsics and the runtime helpers the linked archive classes `Leaf` do not count. This is read off the emitted blocks, so a field read call or a rep-keyed element read the facts lower to plain loads no longer costs the loop its poll (`loop_may_allocate` answers from the HIR, where both are calls). A poll-free nest is limited to 65 536 back edges per entry (trip bound times the nested poll-free loops); a loop past that, a loop with no trip proof and a loop with any collecting call keep their polls. `PERRY_FACTS_BOUNDED_LOOPS=0` keeps every poll; `PERRY_LOOP_POLL_DIAG=1` prints the verdict per loop with a trip proof (`[perry loop poll]`). Check mode keeps the polls and counts the iterations of every loop with a trip proof against its bound (`loop trip bound`; negative control `PERRY_FACTS_SABOTAGE=looptrip`).
