Whole-program facts tolerate `-0` where no use can see it (RFC §10.3 as amended, I32 at rest, PR D): a product such as `color * kind` that may be `0 * negative` keeps its integer interval, and so can be I32, when every use of the value is proven insensitive to the sign of a zero (compares, keys, masks, `Math.abs`, copies); a division, `%`, `Object.is`, `Math.sign`/`min`/`max`/`atan2`, `**`, a native's argument, printing, JSON, an escape or any use the analysis does not classify keeps it a Number. Int demand now also flows through `?? 0` and the other value filters, a summarized call's result, and between an array's element-write, element-read and element-fact nodes. Check mode admits a tolerated `-0` at the int32 witnesses and reports a sign-observing operation that reads a tolerated local. `PERRY_FACTS_ZERO_SIGN=0` restores the strict rule (a possible `-0` is ⊤); `PERRY_FACTS_FILTER_DEMAND=0` keeps the tolerance without the new demand paths; `PERRY_FACTS_ZERO_SIGN_WHY=<template>` prints why a slot's `-0` counts as observed.
