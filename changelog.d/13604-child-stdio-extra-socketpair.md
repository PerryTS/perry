Extra `stdio` pipes (index 3 and up) on `child_process.spawn` were one-way pipes, so a child that read or wrote the descriptor in the other direction failed with `EBADF`, and `child.stdio[n]` could only be read. They are now Unix socketpairs like in Node: `child.stdio[n]` is a duplex stream whose `write` and `end` accept callbacks and emit `finish` (`end` half-closes the socket so the child sees EOF), and the child can use the same descriptor for both directions.
