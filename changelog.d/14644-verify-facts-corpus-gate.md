New gate script `scripts/test-verify-facts-corpus.py --perry <perry> --out <dir>`: check mode (`PERRY_VERIFY_FACTS=1`) over the parity corpora, by default `tests/parity/language` and `tests/parity/node-suite`. It exits 1 when a program reports a false proof of the whole-program facts (run rc 86 or a `PERRY_FALSE_PROOF` line) and lists compile failures without judging them. node-suite is part of the gate because it reaches builtin models that language does not: it found the two false int32 proofs fixed in #14639 and #14641.
