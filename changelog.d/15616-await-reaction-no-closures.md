`await` on a pending promise no longer allocates a pair of resume closures. Following ECMA-262 Await steps 3-9, the awaited promise's reaction slot now holds the suspended async activation itself (its step function, its result promise and the activation token), and settlement queues the activation's resume job directly. An await chain runs 23 % fewer instructions per operation, and nextjs-hello runs about 1.2 % fewer instructions per request on `/` and `/api/hello`. While `async_hooks` promise hooks or `v8.promiseHooks` are active, or when the reaction slot is already taken, the await keeps the closure-pair reaction.
