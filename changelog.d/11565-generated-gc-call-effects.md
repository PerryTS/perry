**GC call effects now come from a generated, CI-checked table (RFC deferred collection, S1).** `classify_direct_callee` no longer uses the hand-kept allowlist. It reads `crates/perry-codegen/src/gc_effects/{linux-x86_64,macos-aarch64,windows-x86_64}.tsv` and takes the most conservative class of the three.

- **Generator.** The tables are generated by `scripts/gc_call_effects/callgraph.py` from a symbol-level call graph of the linked runtime/stdlib archives. Every seed, cut and exemption, each with its reason, is in `seeds.txt`. `why.py` prints the witness path to the tainting seed.
- **CI.** `gc-call-effects-linux` and `gc-call-effects` (macOS aarch64, Windows through `cargo xwin`) regenerate each table from freshly built archives. They fail on any difference, with the witness path and the regenerated table as an artifact. `lint` runs a self-test that plants a `js_proxy_get` call in a leaf helper across ELF, Mach-O and COFF objects and requires the check to go red.
- **#11522.** The graph proves that `js_array_length` (Proxy `get` trap), `js_object_alloc_class_inline_keys*` and `js_object_get_own_field_or_undef` reach JS. They are no longer leaves or alloc-only.
- **#11523.** 23 former `CannotCollect` helpers reach `flush_deferred_gc_request` through a `GcRootRegistryGuard` drop, among them `js_gc_note_slot_layout*`, the typed-feedback guards and `js_closure_set_capture_*`. They are now `AllocOnly`: statepoints under today's runtime.
- **`root_reload`.** Its `NON_COLLECTING` list must now agree with the table.
- **Cost, measured.** On benchmarks/suite plus the 60 largest gap tests, relocations rise +23 %. The #11523 demotions account for more than all of it. Everything else drops −7.8 %: new leaves include `js_clear_exception`, `js_try_end`, `js_eh_try_push`, `js_iter_result_get_done`, `js_inline_arena_state` and `js_eq`. Runtime instructions are flat.
