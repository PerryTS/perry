Per-unit facts: an own getter of a published object literal is a contract function of its owner, as the literal's methods are. A reader runs it on its stand-in of the literal and gets the result its contract describes instead of an unknown value, so `lib.item.kind` through another module's getter is proven as in whole-program mode. The layout contract now says per key whether it holds a contract getter, a setter only, or a getter no contract describes (that one keeps the stub). A contract function that another unit runs on a receiver now has that run's result checked by its contract, so a method or getter returning `this` no longer hides a reader's store into the literal.
