Typed-array loop regions can prove counter indices bounded by a proven view's current `length`, including another view, inclusive upper bounds and integer subtraction. Entry guards and re-checks load both lengths directly from their headers without invariant metadata. The sealed-buffer and inline-storage proofs remain required; exposing, detached and resizable views retain their existing generic fallback. A typed-array annotation alone no longer licenses hoisting a potentially observable length getter. Added Node parity coverage and codegen/IR assertions for unchecked guarded copies.
