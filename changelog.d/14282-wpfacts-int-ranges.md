Performance: whole-program facts carry an integer interval on every Number node: literals, `+ - * %`, the bitwise operators, `++`/`--`, unary `-`/`~` and `Math.abs/min/max/sign/floor/ceil/round/trunc/clz32/imul` follow their operands, and a `for` loop's test bounds its counter's reads. `-0`, NaN and infinities are never integers; anything the analysis does not model is unbounded. `PERRY_FACTS_INT_RANGES=0` turns the intervals off.
