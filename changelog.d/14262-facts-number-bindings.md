Performance: a parameter or `let` the whole-program facts prove holds only Numbers (`number_inputs`: every write, the `undefined` of a missing argument or a bare declaration, and unknown callers counted) takes the raw-double lowering and owns no root slot. A parameter the body never rebinds or mutates converts an INT32 box to its plain double at entry and is a Number by construction; a `let` only answers the Number query for its reads, so a write whose operand may not be a Number keeps its guarded `+`. A facts-verification build checks every consumed claim at the parameter entry and at each store. `PERRY_FACTS_NUMBER_LOCALS=0` turns the proof off.
