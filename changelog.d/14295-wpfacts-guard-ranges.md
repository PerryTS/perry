Performance: whole-program facts narrow a local's integer interval by the comparisons that guard code: `if`/`else` branches, `while` bodies, the right operand of `&&`/`||`, the arms of `?:`, and everything after an `if` whose branch always returns, throws, breaks or continues. A call to a one-expression predicate function (`onBoard(x)`) implies its body's comparisons of the argument. Strings of at most one code unit (`charAt`, one-character literals) carry a code interval that comparisons with a one-character literal narrow, so `Number(c)` of a tested digit is that digit's value. A counter past 2^53 stays a proven integer. Each method call exports its arguments' intervals at the call, and an index the call site proves a non-negative int32 goes to the indexed clone's raw body with no guard (checked under `PERRY_VERIFY_FACTS`, fact `index argument`). Also fixed: a computed result returned through a summarized function kept no interval. `PERRY_FACTS_SITE_ARGS=0` turns the call-site intervals off.
