Performance: a call of an indexed method (`$idx_u31` clone) whose index arguments the call site proves are Numbers selects the clone or the generic body with an inline saturating-convert round trip and sign test, instead of entering the public entry's exact-i32 guard. `-0` is the index 0; NaN, fractions and values outside the i32 range take the generic body. `PERRY_NUMBER_INDEX_SPLIT=0` keeps the public entry.
