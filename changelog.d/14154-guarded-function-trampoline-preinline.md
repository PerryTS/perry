Performance: a function's guarded public trampoline and its specialized clone (`f$spec_*`) are admitted to the pre-statepoint inliner when the clone's lowered IR fits the guarded-specialization budget, as method trampolines already were. A `preserve_none` clone is not admitted, and an admitted clone stays under the pre-inline growth bound on its direct call sites. A one-line helper called with an unproven argument (`onBoard(sq)` in the chess engine) was an out-of-line call to the `noinline` trampoline, a tag test, a second call and a boxed-boolean return. Chess search at 250k nodes: 76.4 G to 74.1 G instructions (-3.1%); perft 4 -4.1%. `PERRY_SPEC_INLINE=0` keeps both out of line.
