A sparse Array (a store far past the end, `a.length = n` or `new Array(n)` above 1 000 000) is no longer read or moved as `length` raw slots of its short store: `concat`, `splice`, `toReversed`, `toSorted`, `toSpliced`, `with`, `at`, `includes`, the numeric `indexOf` and `flatMap` returned raw heap words as elements, crashed or corrupted the heap; they now read the present elements only. A far element is no longer lost when `push`, `unshift`, `splice` or a store just past the end grows the Array over it, and `flat` keeps far elements.
