Every native namespace has one declaration beside its module: its owner, its other names and its members in Node's order. `Object.keys` reads that one list, so the hand key tables and the 89-arm match are gone, thirteen namespaces no longer leak an internal `__module__` key, sixteen namespaces list their members in Node's order, and `crypto.Certificate.prototype` is a real prototype. The module vtable is a fact of the namespace shape; a by-name lookup exists only in programs with a dynamic `require`. The realm-diff gate compares each namespace's member order with Node's.
