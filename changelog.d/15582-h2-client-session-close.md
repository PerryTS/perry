An HTTP/2 client session emits `'close'` from its socket's close callback, as Node does: `session.destroy()` now emits it, `session.close()` emits it once the socket has closed, and sessions destroyed in one turn close newest first, each followed by its ticks. Id-routed turnloop sockets (the `ws` client, the fetch client, the HTTP/2 client) now deliver their close in the close-callbacks phase, like `net` sockets.
