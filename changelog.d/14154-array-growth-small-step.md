Performance: array growth adds at least 16 slots (`max(2 * capacity, capacity + 16)`), so a fresh `[]` filled by a loop reallocates at 4 and 20 elements instead of at 4, 8, 16 and 32. Each reallocation costs about 800 instructions (allocate, copy, forwarding stub) against about 55 for an in-capacity push, and 16 pushes into `[]` spent 64% of their time in two of them. Large arrays still double.
