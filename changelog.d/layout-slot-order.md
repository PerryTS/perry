Layout descriptors now separate key order from storage slots. A runtime declaration can store a key in another slot with `ATOM @ slot`, as long as the slots are a permutation of the key positions; other declarations keep their positions. The slot lives in the key's attribute word, so a reader that loads the key's attributes gets its slot from the same load. Enumeration and reflection follow the key order; reads, writes, caches, copies and the ordered overflow table use the slot. Keys added later take their position as their slot.
