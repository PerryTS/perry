Buffer numeric reads (`buf.readInt32BE(off)` and the other `read*` methods) now follow `Buffer.prototype` as Node does. A call site the whole-program facts prove reads the built-in reader (no write in the program, `eval` or `new Function` included, can reach that member of `Buffer.prototype`, and the receiver holds no own property of the name) compiles to the inline native load with no check. Every other site on a local Buffer is the ordinary method call, which honours a patched `Buffer.prototype`; a read on a guarded Buffer parameter at such a site tests `Buffer.prototype`'s own shadow bit in its admission check and makes that same call once the prototype was written. Interpreted code refuses the proof for a member of a builtin prototype that is not locked. Only a proven site keeps a Buffer read out of the loop length and index-bound facts and out of the sealed-buffer exposure analysis, because a patched reader may shrink an array or take the buffer's storage. The facts now model `Buffer.alloc`, `Buffer.allocUnsafe` and `Buffer.from` results as Buffers, and the module-wide syntactic scan for reader assignments is gone.
