`RegExp.prototype.exec` with capture groups costs fewer instructions. Capture slots are built in place, and `parseInt` reads a short string from its own bytes. When the automaton answers, the search takes no capture poll and runs its one-start capture pass in the view it already opened. perex 0.1.19 checks the capture walk's register window once per walk, not once per write. On js-compiled 32-regex, one exec goes from 4,852 to 4,229 instructions (−12.8 %). Answers, `lastIndex` handling and startup are unchanged.
