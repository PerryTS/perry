**`Expect: 100-continue` is answered when the head arrives (#11560).** The turnloop HTTP/1 connection dispatches a request only at its end and sent `100 Continue` only after dispatch, so a client withholding its body for the interim response deadlocked (`test_http_100_continue_5080` timed out since 09-23). The interim response now goes out as soon as the head is decoded; a `'checkContinue'` listener still gets the request and its `writeContinue()` sends nothing twice.
