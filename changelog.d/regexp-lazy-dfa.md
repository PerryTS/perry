### Performance

- **RegExp searches ask Perex's lazy automaton for a match's bounds before running the backtracking evaluator** (perex 0.1.13). Every program the automaton can read exactly (no backreferences, lookaround or properties of strings) carries a small automaton cache in its program cell, sized once at compilation from the program (its minimum plus 4 KiB; none past 256 KiB). `test`, `exec`, `replace`, `replaceAll`, `split`, `match`, `matchAll` and `search` get the match's span, or no match, from the automaton; only a search that needs capture groups beyond the whole match runs the evaluator, from the span's start, where it finds the same match first. The evaluator still answers programs the automaton cannot read, searches whose cache declines, non-ASCII subjects searched from a later position or in a loop that continues from the previous position, and capture searches of sticky or start-anchored programs. No code is generated per pattern. A `split(/\s+/)` of a 90-character string with no spaces costs 8.7K instructions instead of 57K, a `/\d{3}-\d{4}/.test` over 1 KB of text 5.5K instead of 165K.
