feat(gc): the collector now COMPACTS the old generation while the program is idle (#9644). The idle reclaim (#9589) sweeps at idle, which finds dead old-gen objects and puts them on the old-gen free list — but the free list lives inside partially-occupied blocks and a non-moving sweep never empties one, so the memory stays in the process. Measured on the compiled claude-code TUI: 50 MB of a 93.6 MB old gen sat on that free list across two reducer fulls with the live-block count unchanged at 130. The park site now also runs a synchronous moving minor with old-page defrag selected when the free list has grown past 8 MiB and a quarter of old-gen occupancy, after a reducer full has refreshed the page accounting, at most once every 30 s, backing off when a compaction fails to release 4 MiB. It declines to start when a wake is already pending (the collection cannot be sliced) and reports the pause it cost (`pause_us_max`) rather than assuming one. Two pieces of routing make it possible: the collection asks for the non-copying fallback, because old-page defrag is only selected there and the copying fast path is otherwise eligible on every minor of this workload; and it arms defrag selection for its own collection only — `PERRY_GC_OLD_DEFRAG` stays opt-in for allocation-triggered collections (#7917), since what changed is that idle time can pay for compaction, not that the throughput path should. New trigger kind `idle_compact`; `[gc-idle-compact]` counters under `PERRY_GC_DIAG=1`; kill switch `PERRY_GC_IDLE_COMPACT=0`.
