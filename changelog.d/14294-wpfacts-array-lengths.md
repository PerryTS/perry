Performance: whole-program facts track the length of each program array literal. It stays the literal's element count until something may move it: `push`/`pop`/`splice` and the other length-changing builtins, a `length` or computed-key store, an index store outside the birth length, `Object.assign` or `defineProperty` into it, an escape, or a hard marker. A `for` loop bounded by `x.length` bounds its counter, and `x.length` of a local that only ever holds such arrays compiles to the constant (for-of over a constant table among them). `PERRY_FACTS_ARRAY_LENGTHS=0` turns the lengths off; `PERRY_FACTS_CONST_LENGTH=0` keeps the reads.
