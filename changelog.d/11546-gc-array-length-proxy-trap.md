### Fixed

- **GC: `js_array_length` and `js_array_push_f64` are no longer treated as non-collecting (#11522).** Both were classified allocate-but-never-reenter (`AllocNoReentry`), and `js_array_length` was also listed as non-collecting in `root_reload.rs` and `scripts/gc_root_dominance_check.py`. Neither is: their #5135 arm resolves a Proxy typed as an array (immer drafts) through the user's `get`/`set` traps, and their array-like-object arms run getters, `valueOf` and `array_object_method`. Under `PERRY_GC_SAFEPOINT_ONLY` the calls were emitted as `"gc-leaf-function"`, so a moving minor inside a trap left the caller holding from-space pointers (`live.items` printed as `[object Object]`).
  - `js_array_length` is split. Its plain-array fast lane is the new runtime export `js_array_length_leaf` (header reads only, `-1` on a miss, `CannotCollect`). Codegen's `expr::array_length::emit_array_length_i32` calls the leaf and branches to `js_array_length`, now `Unknown`, only on a miss. That keeps the common case free of statepoints and root reloads in every mode. All nine emission sites use the helper, and the leaf replaces `js_array_length` in the root-reload and dominance-checker non-collecting lists.
  - `js_array_push_f64` is now `Unknown`. `AllocNoReentry` only affects codegen under the research-only `PERRY_GC_SAFEPOINT_ONLY` contract, and push is in neither default-mode list, so default codegen is unchanged. `js_array_push_u31_with_length` keeps its classification: it answers null for exactly these receivers.
  - Re-audited `js_object_alloc_class_inline_keys*` and `js_object_get_own_field_or_undef` by walking their full call graphs. Neither reaches user JS. The allocator collects only on its arena slow path, and only as a non-moving, forced-conservative collection, which is the `AllocNoReentry` contract. The classifications stand, and the comments now state what was walked.
  - Regression test `crates/perry/tests/gc_array_length_proxy_trap_11522.rs` compiles with `PERRY_GC_SAFEPOINT_ONLY=1` and runs under `PERRY_GC_FORCE_EVACUATE=1 PERRY_GC_VERIFY_EVACUATION=1`. It asserts node's output and that a copying minor relocated objects. It has two phases: phase 1 collects inside the push's `length` read, phase 2 inside the length helper's. A survivor that has moved once is promoted and does not move again, so one phase only ever tests whichever helper collects first. Both halves were sabotage-checked independently: restoring `js_array_length` to its old lists fails phase 2 only, and restoring `js_array_push_f64` to `AllocNoReentry` fails phase 1 only.
