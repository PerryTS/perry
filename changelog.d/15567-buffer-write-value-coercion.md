`Buffer.prototype.writeInt8`/`writeUInt16LE`/`writeFloatLE`/`writeDoubleBE`, the other fixed-width writers, `writeIntLE`/`writeUIntBE` and `writeBigInt64LE`/`writeBigUInt64BE` handle their arguments as Node does: the value is coerced with `+value` (so `"7"` and `{ valueOf: () => 5 }` are written as 7 and 5 instead of 0, and a BigInt throws `TypeError`), the BigInt writers reject a Number and range-check their value, and an invalid value, offset or `byteLength` throws Node's `ERR_OUT_OF_RANGE` / `ERR_INVALID_ARG_TYPE` with its message (`The value of "value" is out of range. It must be >= -128 and <= 127. Received 200`).
