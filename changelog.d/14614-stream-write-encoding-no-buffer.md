`process.stdout.write(text, 'hex' | 'base64' | 'base64url')` (and `process.stderr.write`, `buf.write`, `buf.fill`, `buf.indexOf` with such an encoding) decode the text into plain bytes; they no longer allocate a temporary Buffer for it. Output is unchanged. With check mode's family claim on (`PERRY_VERIFY_FACTS=1` and the instrumented runtime), a program that only writes hex or base64 text to stdout no longer reports a false proof with verdict `family`.
