`String.prototype.split` and `matchAll` with a RegExp subclass separator construct the splitter and the matcher through the subclass: the constructor's `@@species` is read as an observable Get that walks the constructor's prototype chain. A function's symbol bag answers a RegExp protocol symbol read only for its own keys, and a proxy constructor's trap runs. Previously an absence over the bag counted as the function's, so the intrinsic RegExp and its builtin exec were used and a subclass exec never ran for split or matchAll.
