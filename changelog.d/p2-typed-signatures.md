Typed signatures (DESIGN-p2, RFC §10 P2): whole-program facts give each function one representation-typed signature, and codegen emits it as `F$sig`/`M$sig` (registers in their signature representation, raw i32 return) behind a boxed public entry. Typed call sites enter `$sig` directly. A tier `$sig` subsumes (spec_abi, typed_abi, `$pshape`, `$pshape_args`, index clones) is dropped for that function only, and only where the signature is at least as precise. `PERRY_FACTS_TYPED_SIGNATURES=0` turns it off; then codegen is byte-identical to before. Typed bodies are registered as their function's frame like spec clones, so `Error.stack` names them (on x86_64 Linux they live in `perry_js_text`). HIR now sets `byte_end` on calls in every compile, so inlined and for…of copies of one source call join in the call-site check.
