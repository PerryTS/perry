wpfacts: accessors run where they are read. A summarized getter or setter now runs on the receiver of the read or store that reached it (before, its body saw no receiver). Per unit, another unit's class contract names its prototype getters: a read under the key runs the owner's getter and finds an unknown value instead of `undefined` only, and the owner lets what its getters return leave beyond any contract. Per-unit facts are no longer stronger than whole-program facts when a getter sits on a path a callee lets escape.
