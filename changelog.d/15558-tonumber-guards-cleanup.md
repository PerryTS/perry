The local BigInt checks in front of ToNumber (Atomics, DataView, typed-array stores, Temporal, `Intl`, the Math builtins and others) are gone now that ToNumber itself throws for a BigInt, and `js_math_to_number` is folded into `js_number_coerce`. Storing a BigInt into a Number typed array (`Float64Array.from([1n])`) now reports `Cannot convert a BigInt value to a number`, as Node does.
