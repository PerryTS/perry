Per-unit facts: a method called on what a described parameter reaches
(`context.factory.createIdentifier(..)`, where the callers' values sit beside
the parameter's marker: a closed function, a run method's parameter) is a
read of the method's key, not an escape of the receiver. The call runs on the
values beside the marker, whose contracts carry it to their units. Before,
the escape made every caller report what it passed, and the census broke the
passed class, its method and the method's result layout. A closed-function
fixture now converges in 2 rounds instead of 4 with no broken layout; the
whole program is unchanged and per unit never proves more than whole.
