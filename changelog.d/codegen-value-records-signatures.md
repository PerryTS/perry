With `PERRY_FACTS_VALUE_RECORDS=1` a value record crosses a typed call as its lanes (the record `$sig` ABI): a parameter the facts give `Rep::Record` is one register per layout word (a plain `double` for a Number field) plus a presence register when it may be `undefined`, and a record result is `[1 + words x double]`, which LLVM returns in registers or through `sret` into the caller's frame. Neither side births an object: the argument is the caller's field locals, the callee's parameter is its field locals, `return` returns the result lanes, and the call writes them into the destination's field locals. wpfacts gives a signature `Record` slots (`Enc::Record`) when every source call of the body is one of its typed calls in its own module (otherwise the slot stays Boxed and receives a copy), and no longer rejects a record at such a slot; a call that is no typed call of one body still rejects (`R5UntypedCall`). A typed call of a record `$sig` that cannot enter it is a compile error, and the boxed entry of such a function admits only `undefined` in a record slot: anything else is a false proof (`value record boxed entry`, rc 86 in check mode, a trap otherwise). `PERRY_FACTS_RECORD_SIGNATURES=0` restores the previous rejection.
