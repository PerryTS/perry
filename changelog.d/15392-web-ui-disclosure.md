Add `Disclosure(title)` to `perry/ui`: a section whose children, attached with `widgetAddChild`, show when the reader expands it. The web target renders `<details>`/`<summary>`; native backends show a `Section`, always expanded and with that widget's limits (on macOS its children are not laid out, reordered or cleared; on iOS, tvOS, visionOS and Android its title counts as the first child; on GTK4 its children are not reordered; on watchOS its title is not drawn). On the web target, `widgetReorderChild` no longer counts a `Section`'s title as its first child and `widgetClearChildren` no longer removes it.
