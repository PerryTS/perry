`Object.prototype`, `Function.prototype` and `Array.prototype` are born on static layouts that list every builtin they hold, so a property read through any of them is answered by its layout: a function or array read that misses its own properties continues through its builtin prototype in the unknown-receiver probe instead of the by-name walk (an absent `obj.constructor.isBuffer` is a few loads). `Object.prototype` now owns the Annex B accessor helpers (`__defineGetter__` and friends) in V8 key order.
