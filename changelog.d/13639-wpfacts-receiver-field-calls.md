Per-unit wpfacts run a method call made through a field of a published class's receiver (`this.svc.onCreate(this)`) in every unit that holds such an instance, on what that instance holds in the field, so the receiver no longer escapes just by being handed to that call. A constructor's field initializers are part of its contract, so a subclass in another unit now sees their stores through `super()`. A constructor no longer runs on the class's receiver token.
