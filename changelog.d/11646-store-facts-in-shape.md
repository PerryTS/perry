Two per-object store facts are now part of shape identity. They are an object's "own slots are not proven plain data" mark and the Array-subclass numeric proof, and they live as the shape kinds `OrdinaryUnmarked` and `OrdinaryNumericProof`. A store is admitted on the shape compare alone: the emitted store IC drops its separate kind, class and class-less checks (`.text` −0.5% on tsc, −1.26% on Zod). Setting or clearing a fact moves the object to its twin shape; minting the twin copies the source shape's facts and never reads its keys.
