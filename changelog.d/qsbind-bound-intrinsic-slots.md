Resolve bound Function.prototype.call/apply adapters at bind time. Bound function shapes type a traced invocation-operand slot while preserving the original target and name/length snapshots. Eligible calls enter the known body's ABI directly, using the caller's argument slice or a short packed-array stack snapshot, without repeated receiver-rebind or generic callee probes. Exotic targets, partial arguments and methods that capture a rebindable receiver retain the shared forwarding mechanism. Ordinary binds retain their constant five-slot birth and installer; earlier shape-kind ordinals remain unchanged. The direct body entry is limited to operands whose receiver behavior was proved at bind time. Resolved six-slot birth is isolated from the ordinary bind allocation frame, and ordinary binds do not read the invocation operand during intrinsic resolution. Bind shares its immutable target descriptor across metadata reads and adapter admission; only native builtin targets enter intrinsic resolution. Shape kinds occupy a contiguous four-bit field; the birth-count high bit moves into unused space without changing the record size or any semantic fact. Ordinary body birth skips the resolved-layout comparisons through the existing bound-function code sentinel.
