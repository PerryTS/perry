**Startup:** runtime-only macOS binaries no longer hard-link Security/CoreFoundation/SystemConfiguration/libiconv/libresolv/libobjc — their imports are exclusively libSystem, and the CoreFoundation+libobjc initializers alone cost ~1.8 ms of every launch (measured: hello world 5.0 → 3.2 ms class on an M-series host). Stdlib/UI/plugin/geisterhand links keep the full list; a runtime-only program that genuinely needed one fails at link time, never at runtime.
