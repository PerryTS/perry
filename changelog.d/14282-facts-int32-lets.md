Performance: a `let` whose whole-program interval lies in the int32 range takes the integer (i32) slot when the existing profitability rule chooses it, converting stored doubles with a plain `fptosi`; the spec-ABI call-site judge counts such bindings as integers. `PERRY_VERIFY_FACTS=1` witnesses every store into one (fact `int32 input`), and `PERRY_FACTS_SABOTAGE=range` is its negative control. `PERRY_FACTS_INT32_LOCALS=0` admits none.
