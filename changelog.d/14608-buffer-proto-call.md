`Buffer.prototype` methods called with an explicit receiver (`Buffer.prototype.copy.call(src, dst)`, `.apply`, `.bind(buf)()`, `Reflect.apply(Buffer.prototype.readUInt8, buf, [0])`) now run the method on that receiver, for a Buffer, a plain `Uint8Array` or a subclass instance, as in Node; before they returned `undefined` and did nothing. A receiver that is not a `Uint8Array` view throws a TypeError.
