Archived R8 experiment: admit primitive true and either sign of numeric zero to existing bounded JSON.stringify output paths while preserving boxed coercions and the separate lazy shortcut. Fresh-main comparisons show about 8.9–9.7× faster small-record zero-spacing calls, but ordinary stringify and key-list controls show small slowdowns. This version is not qualified for landing under the no-regression requirement. Full CPU/RSS tables, 1,656 samples, exact-build provenance, copying-GC checks, unsuppressed static findings, and separately reproduced baseline correctness gaps are recorded in benchmarks/json_performance/INERT_SPACER_R8.md.
