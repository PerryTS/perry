Whole-program facts carry an int-demand representation for `let` slots (int demand crosses call bindings and summary instances). They also prove element reads of program Arrays that never change length and never gain named properties. A proven read loads its slot directly, with no receiver, type, forwarding or prefix test, and with no bound check when the key's interval lies inside the length. `new Array(k)` with a literal `k` records its birth length. `PERRY_WP_REP_P1=0` and `PERRY_WP_ELEMENT_READS=0` turn each part off. `PERRY_VERIFY_FACTS=1` checks every proven read at runtime.
