**The v8 serializer and deserializer now expose their operations through the canonical constructor prototypes.** They were reachable only through the native-module vtable, so `v8.Serializer.prototype.writeHeader` did not exist, `typeof s.writeHeader` was `undefined`, and `Object.getPrototypeOf(s) === v8.Serializer.prototype` was false. `node_v8/serde_prototypes.rs` now owns the methods — one shim per implemented operation (`writeHeader`, `writeValue`, `releaseBuffer`, `writeUint32`, `writeUint64`, `writeDouble`, `writeRawBytes` and their `read*` counterparts), each validating its receiver through the module brand and throwing `Illegal invocation` otherwise — and `attach` installs them on a constructor's prototype, with the `Default*` subclasses linking to their parent's prototype instead of duplicating the table. `build_v8_instance` links the receiver's `[[Prototype]]` to that same prototype, so ordinary lookup finds them. The node-suite case `test_gap_v8_serde_prototype_methods.ts` now matches Node byte for byte (method and prototype identity, method lengths, the deserializer's prototype, and the brand `TypeError`), and a runtime unit test pins that `attach` installs on the prototype of the constructor it is handed and that the object the `v8` namespace exposes is that same constructor. This is the prototypes half of the work: the object-construction rooting rework and the ABI change that enables `Reflect`-based dynamic aliases are not carried.
