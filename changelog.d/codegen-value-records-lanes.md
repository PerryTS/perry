With `PERRY_FACTS_VALUE_RECORDS=1` the locals that hold a value record's lanes carry their field's facts: a lane of a Number field (wpfacts `FieldRep::F64` or `I32`) is declared `number` and is one of the facts' Number bindings (`number_inputs`), an int32 field's lane one of the int32 bindings (`int32_inputs`), since it holds a Number at all times (an absent record's filler is `0`, and a typed call's absent result now binds the fillers instead of the unwritten result words). Before, every lane local was untyped: a GC root slot spilled at each call, with generic arithmetic and compares on its reads. The boxed program's key-by-key read of a typed call's result is no longer lowered when the call bound the lanes of a never-absent record. `PERRY_VALUE_RECORD_LANE_FACTS=0` (a compile-cache input) leaves the lanes untyped (value records VR-4c).
