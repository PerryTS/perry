The call-graph locality partition of split codegen units is now the default (`PERRY_CODEGEN_UNIT_LOCALITY=0` selects the size partition). With the facts resolving three.js methods to direct calls, a caller and its small callee in one LLVM unit are inlined: three.js workloads run 2.0 to 2.4 % fewer instructions and their `.text` is 0.9 % smaller. Modules that are not split are unaffected.
