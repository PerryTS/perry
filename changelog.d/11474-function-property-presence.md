Fix string-keyed `in` and `Reflect.has` checks on functions to inspect own property presence and follow the actual prototype chain. Inherited Function.prototype members, undefined-valued properties, accessor properties, custom prototypes and proxy `has` traps are now handled without invoking getters. Ordinary objects can also inherit properties through a function prototype.
Also connect generator and async-generator intrinsic constructors and prototypes to their Function parents, making inherited callable methods visible through those chains.
Also fix `fresh_unique_proto_id` minting the same prototype identity for serials 2k and 2k+1: its `...FE` mask dropped bit 0, so two distinct unserialled prototypes could share a shape. The identity is now reduced modulo the band size minus one, which still keeps clear of `PROTO_ID_NULL`.
