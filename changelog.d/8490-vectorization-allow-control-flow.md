ci: `native_owned_typed_views` accepts `control_flow` as a missed-vectorization reason (#8489). Three of its loops began reporting that reason, failing the "Gate native-ABI proof compiler output" step of `compiler-output-regression` — one of `full-suite-gate`'s required jobs.

**Attributed to `40e2ce86d`, "perf(codegen): make -Os the default native optimization level" (#8457).** The Linux window between the last green run of that step (`526e0b502`) and the first red one (`40e2ce86d`) held exactly two commits; `f14a9e262` (#8418) measures clean and `40e2ce86d` measures dirty.

It is **not a codegen regression**. Perry's emitted IR is byte-identical either side of the commit — sha256 `247d4d764e7f…` for this fixture at `526e0b502`, `f14a9e262` and `40e2ce86d` alike. What changed is the `-O` level perry passes to clang (`-O3` → `-Os`, and the `-inlinehint-threshold=850` override dropped); the harness's vectorization-remark pass reuses the compile's own arguments (`_analysis_args_from_metadata`), so the analysis moved to `-Os` with it. At `-Os` LLVM declines the SimplifyCFG speculation, if-conversion and inlining that at `-O3` had flattened these branches into selects before the vectorizer ran, so the loops genuinely carry control flow it cannot if-convert — and vectorizing them would grow code size, which is what `-Os` asks LLVM not to do. `-Os` did not cost vectorization where it is actually required: `vectorized_buffer_transform` still vectorizes 2 loops against a floor of 1.

Corrects two claims in the original report. It is **not Linux-only and not release-only**: the job builds `cargo build -p perry` (a *debug* build), and that same debug build on macOS/arm64 reproduces it exactly — `control_flow` 0 at `526e0b502` and `f14a9e262`, 3 at `40e2ce86d`, matching the Linux counts (`call_instruction` 4, `control_flow` 3, `unknown_trip_count` 1). The earlier "not reproducible on macOS" result came from a `--profile perry-dev` build, which is not what CI runs. It is also **not** "pre-existing before the merge batch" in the sense reported: `40e2ce86d` is an ancestor of the baseline `3627657c7` the batch was measured against, which is why that baseline looked equally red.

The widening therefore stands as the correct fix rather than a temporary unblock: this workload requires no vectorization (`min_vectorized_loops = 0` with an explicit scalar baseline), so the reason list is a change detector, and it now records a deliberate, understood optimization-policy change.

Separately noted while attributing this: between 2026-07-30 and 2026-08-17 the "Gate native-ABI proof compiler output" step **never executed**. The earlier "Gate native-region proof compiler output" step failed first, and a failing `run:` step aborts the remaining steps in the job — so this gate was dark for roughly three weeks and only started reporting once the region-proof step went green on 2026-08-18. That is the "a gate runs but its subject never did" shape from CLAUDE.md, and it is why the failure looked like it appeared out of nowhere two days later.
