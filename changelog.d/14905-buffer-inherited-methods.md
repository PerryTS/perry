Reading a `%TypedArray%.prototype` method that `Buffer.prototype` does not override as a value from a `Buffer` (`buf.join`, `buf.map`, `buf.forEach`, `buf.reduce`, `buf.sort`, `buf.findLast`, `buf.with`, `buf.toReversed`, …) now resolves it through the Buffer's prototype chain. It used to answer `undefined`. As a result `typeof buf.map === "function"`, and `Array.prototype.toString.call(buf)` / `Uint8Array.prototype.toString.call(buf)` join the bytes as in Node.
