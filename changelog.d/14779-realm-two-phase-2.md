Runtime and compiler: a realm that is built by need, behind `PERRY_REALM_TWO_PHASE=1` (read by the compiler; off by default, and off is unchanged). The population of the realm's global object has two phases, declared in one place (`perry-abi/src/realm_phase.rs`): the core at the first touch, and the rest (the weak collections, `Proxy`, `FinalizationRegistry`, `Atomics`, `Intl`, `WebAssembly`, `Temporal` and six `perf_hooks` constructors) only when something can observe it: the global object as a value, one of those names, an undeclared name, or any store. A compiled static read of a core name takes its receiver from the new core entry (`js_get_global_core`); every other read and every store takes the full entry (`js_get_global_this`), and the runtime's internal births of a phase-B family complete the realm first. Keys, their order, attributes and identities of the global object are those of the one-pass realm (the global is born on its static layout; phase B adds no key). With the switch on, chess start-up (`evaluate 1`) falls from 30.54 M to 28.30 M instructions. Check mode (`PERRY_VERIFY_FACTS=1`) poisons unbound phase-B slots and reports any compiled global read that sees one.
