A module with 64 or more callables lowers them on worker threads only when a CPU slot is free at that moment, and the workers lacked three pieces of the module thread's state: the body classifications of dead-body removal, the constructor-layout store plans and the closed-target notes. The same compile could therefore emit a proven-dead body as a trap or in full, a store as the constructor-layout or the generic one, and pre-inline fewer closed call targets, depending on the host's load. Workers now get all three (and, in a `PERRY_CHECK_CONTRACTS=1` build, the module's name and line starts for witness sites), so a compile gives the serial result whatever the number of threads. Both forms were valid code; the binary is now the smaller, serial one every time.
