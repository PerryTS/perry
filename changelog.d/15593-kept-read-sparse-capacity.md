An element read of a holey Array tests its index against the Array's store, not only its length. A fresh `new Array(k)` longer than 1,000,000 has length `k` over an empty store, and a loop over it (`for (let i = 0; i < a.length; i++) a[i]`, `for (const x of a)`, a reverse `while`, with or without calls in the body) no longer reads past the allocation and crashes (#15608).
