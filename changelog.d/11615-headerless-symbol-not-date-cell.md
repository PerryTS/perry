**Runtime: a `Symbol.for` or well-known symbol no longer classifies as a Date or Temporal cell (#11558).** These symbols are Box-leaked `SymbolHeader`s with no `GcHeader`, but `is_date_cell_addr` and `is_temporal_cell_addr` read `obj_type` from the word in front of the address. Under mimalloc that word is the tail of the previous block. When it read 17 (`GC_TYPE_DATE_CELL`), a property read on the symbol took the Date arm of the generic read, and its own-property probe dereferenced the symbol's `SYMBOL_MAGIC` word as `DateCell.meta`. claude-code 2.1.112's `doctor` SIGSEGV'd in `get_accessor_descriptor`, with `rax=0x153594d42`, while reading `defaultProps` off a React element-type symbol. Both classifiers now screen out registered symbols, the same screen #7850 added for method dispatch. Covered by `date::tests::a_headerless_symbol_behind_a_date_cell_tag_is_not_a_date` and `test-files/test_gap_11558_registered_symbol_property_read.ts`, which recreates the allocator layout from JS; both fail with the fix reverted.
