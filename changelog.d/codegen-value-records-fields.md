With `PERRY_FACTS_VALUE_RECORDS=1` a binding the facts give `Rep::Record` is its fields by value: before lowering, each such `let` becomes one local per layout key (plus a presence local when it may be `undefined`); a field read is its local, a presence test (`=== undefined`, `== null`, truthiness, `typeof`, `&&`, `||`, `??`) reads the presence local, an assignment writes the fields, and every other use, which the facts admit only at a sanctioned boundary or a helper's Boxed parameter, takes a materialized copy born by the template's own literal birth. A binding the rewrite cannot reach completely (captured, a module global, exported, referenced outside the bodies it visits) stays a heap object. Records do not cross program calls yet: a record in a typed call's argument or result is rejected (`R5UntypedCall`, "the record signature ABI is VR-3b"), except at a Boxed helper parameter. Check mode claims each copy at its birth and reports a store into a copy (`value record mutation`); new S0 arm `vrwrite`, parity fixtures `test_vr_fields.ts` and `test_vr_fields_opt.ts`, `PERRY_VALUE_RECORDS_TRACE=1` prints each module's rewrite census (value records VR-3a).
