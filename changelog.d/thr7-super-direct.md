Classes: `super.m(...)` in an instance method of a declared class is a direct call of the holder's method body, proven by the home prototype's static shape and the parent chain's ConstFn holder. Where the program writes `m` or relinks a prototype, the call is guarded by the prototype shadow bits. A failed guard reads the live home parent out of line, in spec order. A non-callable `super.m` throws Node's `(intermediate value).m is not a function`. three-style `super.getP(); this.bone()` goes from 68.6 to 8.0 ns per call (inline ceiling 6.9).
