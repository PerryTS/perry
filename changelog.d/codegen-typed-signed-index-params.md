Performance: a method whose parameters feed element keys gets its typed body (`M$sig`) when whole-program facts prove those parameters int32, whatever their sign. Before, the non-negative index clones (`$idx_u31`) of such a method refused the typed signature unless every index parameter's interval started at 0, so a helper like `set(square, piece)` with a possibly negative key or value kept its double-ABI generic body and its guarded clones. Since the unsigned bound an int32 key is bounded by one unsigned compare, which declines a negative key onto the property path. `PERRY_SIG_SIGNED_INDEX=0` restores the old rule. New probes: `# typed_call` lines in `PERRY_FACTS_DUMP` (each call that resolves to a body with a signature, typed or refused with the reason) and `PERRY_SIG_DIAG=1` (the selector's verdict per method).
