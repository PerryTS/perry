`Event` and `DOMException` are born on first observation (LAZYPROTO P2 batch B3): realm setup allocates only their constructors, and the prototype, its members and the constructors' constants (`Event.NONE` .. `BUBBLING_PHASE`, the 25 `DOMException.*_ERR` codes, declared as the constructors' statics) are installed the first time the family is observed (an instance, a constant, `prototype`, a child such as `CustomEvent`). A program that populates the global object and never touches either family runs about 1.8 M fewer instructions at start-up. `PERRY_LAZY_EVENT_FAMILIES=0` (read by the compiled program) births both at realm setup as before.
