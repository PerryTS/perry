The memory-stability gate's `[gc-trace] default copied minor churn` probe drives its cycles by allocation instead of `gc()`. An explicit `gc()` is a full mark-sweep unless `PERRY_GC_FORCE_EVACUATE` is set, so every cycle reported `collection_kind=full` and `fallback_reason=not_attempted` and the probe's all-minor assertion could never pass; the probe now observes default-policy copying minors.
