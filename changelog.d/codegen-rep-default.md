Performance: `a[k] ?? <number literal>` over a local, parameter or field the whole-program facts give an Array of Numbers (`Rep::Arr` with `elem` f64 or i32, not at rest) tests for `undefined` on the read's miss arm only. A present slot of such an Array is a plain double, never `undefined` or `null`, so the hit (not forwarded, the key below the capacity, the slot no hole) is the slot's value; a hole, a key outside the Array and a key that names a property take the exact generic read with the original key, and its `undefined` or `null` becomes the literal. The generic lowering merged both arms and tested the merged value on every read. Taken for a key the body's range proofs bound to int32 or the facts prove an int32 or a wide key; a read below a dense floor (never `undefined`, no test at all) and an at-rest Array keep their own lowerings. Check mode tests the receiver's claim and the slot before the load ("array rep"; `rep-default-negative` with `PERRY_FACTS_SABOTAGE=arrelem` is its negative control). `PERRY_REP_DEFAULT=0` keeps the generic lowering.
