Resolve `rustc` without consulting `PATH` in the strip-dedup archive tests. Sibling tests in `optimized_libs/tests.rs` overwrite — and briefly remove — the process-global `PATH`, which is shared across test threads, so `Command::new("rustc")` failed with ENOENT whenever they ran concurrently. `cargo test -p perry --bin perry` was failing 6 of 6 runs on `main` at its default thread count.
