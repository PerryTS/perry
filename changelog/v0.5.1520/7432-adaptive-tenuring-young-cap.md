**GC: adaptive tenuring threshold + young-generation-scoped scavenge cap** — fixes the large-live-set scavenge regression from #7377, where `tree.ts` ran 4.6× slower with 1.65× more peak RSS than `PERRY_GC_SCAVENGE=0` because a saturated survivor semispace was re-copied on every collection (4.39 GB / 61 M object-copies across 1427 collections).

Two stacked root causes:

- **The scavenge nursery cap fired on `arena_total_bytes()`** — all generations, and capacity at that. Once old-gen (or simply ever-mapped capacity) crossed 16 MB, every fresh 1 MB Eden block re-crossed the trigger, degenerating the scavenge cadence to once-per-block. Objects were scavenged ~1 MB of allocation after birth (measured 1.05 MB survivors per block — near-zero infant mortality), which is what saturated the survivor space. After warmup this hit every workload: `churn.ts` ran 1672 collections. The cap arm of `gc_budgeted_due_trigger` now fires on Eden + active-survivor occupancy; the adaptive base trigger keeps its historical total-arena basis.
- **A fixed promotion age** (`GC_COPY_PROMOTION_SURVIVALS = 4`) re-copied cohorts that aging cannot filter. The new `gc/tenuring.rs` re-derives the threshold after every copying minor from the Eden survivor influx (`S = min(4, 1 + desired/influx)`, desired = effective cap/16 — HotSpot's adaptive `TenuringThreshold` restated for elastic semispaces), with an instant drop and a debounced one-step rise; the promotion decision stays purely per-object so the copied/promoted counter split remains bit-deterministic (the gc-ratchet's gating contract). A **survival-rate lock** promotes on first copy while ≥90% of a substantial survivor intake keeps surviving its round; an **influx-driven cap scale** grows the effective cap ×2 (max 64 MB) while influx exceeds 4% of it. No new env knob: the loop's neutral state is bit-for-bit the previous fixed behaviour, and small-live-set workloads never leave the 16 MB base.

Measured (macOS arm64): `tree.ts` 48.06 s → 10.26 s, copying volume down 257× (4.39 GB → 17.1 MB — the residue is the first heavy cycle's one-time cohort copy), collections 1427 → 43; `retain.ts` 19.34 s → 6.96 s; `deeplist.ts` 5.28 s → 2.04 s; `churn.ts`/`cycles.ts` (the #7377 wins) unchanged at 4.64 s / 24 MB and 1.21 s / 29 MB. `tree.ts` peak RSS (221 MB vs 103 MB scavenge-off) remains above the scavenge-off arm — that residual is young high-water plus old-gen churn under the pre-existing 48 MB/+32 MB old-reclaim pacing, tracked separately.

The per-cycle GC trace gains `tenuring_survivals`, `eden_live_bytes`, and `survivor_live_bytes`; `PERRY_GC_DIAG=1` reports threshold and cap-scale transitions. New gc-ratchet probe `12_large_live_set.ts` pins the ~100 MB-live-set steady state every existing probe missed.
