**Conformance ratchet:** the gap suite now gates on a generated, committed snapshot (`test-parity/gap_snapshot.json`) instead of the one-directional `known_failures.json` allowlist. CI fails on a new failure *and* on a listed test that starts passing, so a skip-list entry can no longer outlive the bug it tracked (#797). `node_fail`/`skipped` are recorded explicitly rather than dropped from the denominator (#6364). Accept changes with `UPDATE_SNAPSHOT=1 ./scripts/run_gap_tests.sh`.
