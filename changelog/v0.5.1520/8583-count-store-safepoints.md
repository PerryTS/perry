fix(codegen): the RS4GC root-spill estimate (`count_safepoint_sites`, #8583) now counts property/index STORES (`PropertySet`, `PropertyUpdate`, `IndexSet`) as GC safepoints, extending the earlier literal-counting fix. A closed-shape object literal compiles to a constructor that is one long run of `this.field = v` stores, each lowering to a collecting `js_class_field_set_ic` / `js_set_property` call that RS4GC gives a statepoint; with none counted, the constructor's estimate was ~0, it was never spilled, and RS4GC grew one such `__AnonShape_*_constructor` from 34k to 2.28M instructions — overrunning the #8586 per-function budget and refusing the whole module. Reads (`PropertyGet`/`IndexGet`) are deliberately not counted: they frequently inline to a shape-cached load with no call, and counting them would over-spill read-heavy hot loops.
