Fix: a socket whose peer writes and half-closes at once (`socket.end("a")`) no longer ends its own writable side before a `setImmediate` queued by its `'data'` handler runs. turnloop reported the data and the EOF from one turn and Perry delivered both, so `'end'` and the `allowHalfOpen: false` auto-end ran before the check phase, and the immediate's `socket.end(x)` failed with `ERR_STREAM_WRITE_AFTER_END` (intermittently, e.g. `async_hooks/providers/net-data-isolation.ts`). An EOF read behind data is now delivered at the end of the next check phase, as Node's libuv reads it on the following poll; an EOF with no data before it is delivered at once, and a parked EOF is dropped if its socket is closed first. Applies to every turnloop stream (net, TLS, HTTP, WebSocket). `PERRY_NET_DEFER_EOF=0` restores the old order.
