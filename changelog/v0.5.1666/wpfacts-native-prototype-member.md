Whole-program facts: a built-in method read through its home object is the native it names. `Array.prototype.slice`, `Map.prototype.get` or `String.prototype.toUpperCase`, read through the namespace (`F.prototype.K`, directly or kept in a binding), is that prototype's own method when the prototype declares it and decision 250 keeps it final; `.call(recv)`, `.apply(recv, args)` and `.bind(recv)` on it run what `recv.K()` runs when `recv` is of that family, and stay an unknown call otherwise. A shadowed or reassigned namespace binding, a variable that may hold a program object, a key the prototype does not declare, and a member of another family stay unknown, as before.
