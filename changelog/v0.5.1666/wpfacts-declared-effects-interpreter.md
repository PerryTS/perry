Whole-program facts: a built-in that declares its effects where the runtime defines it (`#[realm(.., effects = {..})]`) runs through one interpreter of that declaration in the solver (`builtins/effects.rs`), and the hand-written models of `Array.prototype` push, reverse, every, some, find, indexOf, includes and join are gone: their declarations take over. With them `indexOf`/`includes` convert `fromIndex` (an object's `valueOf` runs, which the old model missed), their results are one primitive kind (a number, a boolean), and `join` converts only the separator. `sort`, `forEach`, `map` and `filter` keep their models for now (the comparator result conversion, an array receiver's numeric index and the species construction of map/filter results are not yet declared).
