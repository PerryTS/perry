Link-time archive dedup now reads each member's native symbol table: every `llvm-nm` call in `strip_dedup` passes `--no-llvm-bc` (only for LLVM-based nm tools, GNU `nm` rejects it). Before, on macOS archives that still carried an `__LLVM,__bitcode` section (unstripped local builds), `llvm-nm` returned the bitcode module's symbols, dedup compared the wrong sets, kept duplicate std/core members and the link printed `duplicate symbol` warnings.
