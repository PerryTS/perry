Cold construction, holes and descriptors keep the rooted path; growth forwarding and cached length are resolved before reading materialized slots. The helper is outlined to preserve the ordinary array dispatcher’s structure.

The complete CPU/RSS evidence in `benchmarks/json_performance/OUTLINED_CACHED_READ_R24.md` discloses small plain-stringify costs and the existing validation limitations.
