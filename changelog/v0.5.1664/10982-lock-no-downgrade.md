### Tooling

- Added `scripts/lock_no_downgrade.py`, which refuses a `Cargo.lock` change that moves a resolved registry dependency *backwards*. The check is per consumer: a dependency counts as downgraded only when a consumer present in both locks, at the same version, now resolves it lower. A higher version that left along with all its consumers is reported as a removal and does not fail. A consumer whose own version changed falls back to a name-level comparison, so a consumer's bump can't hide a downgrade it brought in. Pre-releases sort below their release, a comparison that checks zero edges fails instead of passing, and `--vs <ref>` compares that ref's lock with the tree's. Only `--self-test` runs in CI (lint). The real comparison runs at merge time. This came from #10980, where a lock regenerated during a merge put rustls back to 0.23.44 even though the RUSTSEC-2026-0285 bump to 0.23.45 was already in history. (#10980)
