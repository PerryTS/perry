Reconcile `scripts/gc_runtime_root_holders.json` with the collector refactors that landed after its last audit, and advance the two source-pinned contracts it had invalidated (#13495).

The gate was failing on `main` in four independent ways, none of them a new GC risk:

* **11 stale `frontier` pins.** Their holders either became COVERED by a registered scanner (`RECONSTRUCT_CENSUS`, `REMEMBERED_SET_RECONSTRUCTED`, `TEST_ARMED_OVERRIDE`, `GC_LAST_OLD_RECLAIM_IN_USE_BYTES`, `FFI_MUTABLE_ROOT_SCANNERS`, `FFI_NAMED_MUTABLE_ROOT_SCANNERS`, and the three `ITER_RESULT_*` cells, since consolidated into a covered `ITER_RESULT`) or moved module (`GC_STATS`, `CLASSIFIER_VERIFY_SUPPRESSED`). Coverage and a rename are the receipt: the debt pin is deleted, and the two still-uncovered holders are graduated out of the debt list with a researched verdict instead.
* **8 stale `holders` entries.** #13443 folded seven per-counter TLS declarations (`FORWARDED_STUB_MEMBERSHIP_RECOVERIES`, `BLOCK_PERSIST_FORCE_MARKS`, `ADOPTED_BLOCKS`, `HOLE_REBUILD_BLOCKS_SKIPPED`, `BLOCK_SKIP_RECLAIMED_{BLOCKS,OBJECTS,BYTES}`) into `GcStats` fields behind one `GC_STATS` slot, so those declarations no longer exist. `alloc_census.rs:CREDIT` is reached by a registered scanner today.
* **5 new rule-T holders**, each classified rather than pinned as debt: `GC_STATS: RefCell<GcStats>` and `CLASSIFIER_VERIFY_SUPPRESSED: Cell<bool>` are `not_a_gc_pointer` (counters, a duration ring and a cursor; a boolean latch), and `LAST_SKIPPED_BASES`, `DFA_CAPTURE_ANSWERS`, `DFA_CAPTURE_PASSES` are `test_only` (all three sit behind `#[cfg(test)]`).
* **13 source pins** whose content moved under `efd2738fbe` (#13443) and the #13431/#13397/#13457/#13463/#13471/#13472/#13479 slices: 10 in the `PASS1_MARKED` window and 3 under the `INTERN_TABLE` weak-cache contract.

The two pin re-audits are the part that is not bookkeeping. `PASS1_MARKED` is a `Vec<usize>` of real GC header addresses, deliberately untraced, populated at the end of mark propagation and consumed at sweep entry inside one `run_to_completion`; the contract is that nothing between the boundaries allocates a GC object, relocates, collects or runs a JS callback. Both boundaries and both predicates are unchanged, and the window's phases were read rather than assumed: `BlockPersistence` only marks, and the only relocating subphase of `AtomicFinalize` — `MinorPrelude` — is entered solely from the minor branch of the ephemeron clear, while a full cycle goes `WeakProcessing` → `DisableBarrier` → `Sweep`. #13443's const-generic `DIAGNOSTICS` split gates only `trace_phase_*` recording and the read-only honest-pointer and minor-unmarked verifiers. `INTERN_TABLE` still has exactly two production writers: the `publish_intern_entry` funnel, which refuses publication for the whole duration of every open `HeapChange`, and the full clear from `HeapChange::begin` and `gc_realloc` before a `GC_TYPE_STRING` cell can move or be released. Every other slot site in the crate is a read, and #13431's new `AtomTable::probe_from`/`seed_if_absent` writes the separate immutable-image `ATOMS` table.

No runtime behaviour changes: this commit edits gate data only. The inventory file was not reformatted; the round-trip through its JSON encoding is byte-identical.
