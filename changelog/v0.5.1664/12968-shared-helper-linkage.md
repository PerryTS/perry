Split text codegen units now retain shared index-normalization and birth helpers even when every caller is in another unit. Their owning definitions use the same non-discardable `weak_odr` linkage as native construction, preserving cross-module coalescing on ELF and Mach-O. Previously LLVM could drop the sole `linkonce_odr` definition and the executable failed to link with an undefined helper symbol. Added a regression that forces separate owner/caller units, compiles and links all three shared helper kinds, and coalesces a second provider.
