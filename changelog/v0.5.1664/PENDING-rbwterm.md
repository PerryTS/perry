The shared read-before-write check (`collectors/read_before_write.rs`, asked by `typed_locals` and `integer_locals`) now knows which paths cannot complete normally, so a `let x;` written on every path that continues past a `switch`, `if` or `try` is definitely assigned after it and keeps its int32 / typed-local lowering. `return` and `throw` end a path; `break` carries its state to the end of the loop, `switch` or labeled statement it leaves; `continue` goes back to its loop head; a `switch` without `default` also completes when no case is taken, and fallthrough carries a case's state into the next; a `try` completes only through a completing block or catch and then a completing `finally`. Loop bodies are still never assumed to run. In tsc this keeps int32 locals such as `log2Base` in `parsePseudoBigInt` and the checker's `currentKind` (written in every case of a switch whose `default` returns or throws), which #12658's check had dropped.
