On single-threaded WASI, generated code pushes and pops shadow-stack frames inline against the runtime's exported `PERRY_SHADOW_STATE` instead of calling `js_shadow_frame_enter` / `js_shadow_frame_pop` (still the slow arms for buffer growth and bad handles), and clears exactly the frame's slots. A recursive object-tree walk runs 10.7% fewer instructions. Native targets are unchanged.
