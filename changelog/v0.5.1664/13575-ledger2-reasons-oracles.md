Facts dump (`PERRY_FACTS_DUMP` only): ledger follow-ups. Every unknown value contract names the first reason it is unknown and the unit that published it (`why` on the `value-output`, `argument-output` and `contract-input` records; the reason takes no part in contract equality or hashing). The wrapper offsets of CommonJS modules are kept when the dump is on (`W` records), so the ledger maps wrapped-coordinate sites back to source. The per-unit census rounds capture no diagnostics: each unit is solved once more at the fixpoint, level by level as a round would, and its records stream to `<dump>.diag` (appended to the dump) instead of being held every round, so a program of tens of thousands of units fits. `PERRY_FACTS_CONSUMED=<file>` (full compile) lists the sites codegen read a program fact for. A ⊤ whose chain has no recorded seed names where it broke. With the dump off nothing changes: native images and fact fingerprints are unchanged.
