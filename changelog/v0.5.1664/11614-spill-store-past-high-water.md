- **perry-runtime tests**: regression coverage for #11559 (claude-code `-p` reading a tool record's `prompt` method back as a plain object). The root cause, a spill store past the buffer's high-water mark reading the headroom word as the overwritten value, is fixed by #11610. This adds `spill_store_past_high_water_ignores_headroom_bits`, which asserts the collector enumerates a pointer stored past `length` and that the gap is hole-filled, and the gap test `test_gap_gc_11559_spill_headroom_store.ts` (253 bad reads without the fix, no GC knobs needed), registered in the repsel corpus.
