- **perf(gc): the budgeted-cycle membership classifier asks the arena term before the malloc registry** (#13077).
`classifier_valid_object_start` decides membership for every traced edge of a budgeted full cycle: the idle reducer and the pacing fulls. It is a union of a plausible-arena-header term and an exact malloc-registry term, and it probed the registry hash set first even though nearly every edge is an arena object. It now evaluates the arena term first. The answer is unchanged, because a malloc header never satisfies the arena term and the registry is still asked on `Unknown` pages. On the compiled opencode server at idle, each budgeted full over its ~100 MB old heap takes 7% fewer instructions. Test: `gc::tests::classifier_membership`.
