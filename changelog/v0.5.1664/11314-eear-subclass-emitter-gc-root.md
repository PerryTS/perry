`sub.asyncResource.eventEmitter === sub` now holds for an `EventEmitterAsyncResource` subclass after a moving collection (#11258). The native `AsyncResource` backing (a never-freed `Box`) stored the subclass's `this` in its `event_emitter` word as a raw heap address. No collector ever saw that word, so a copying minor that moved the emitter left it pointing at the stale from-space copy.

`set_async_resource_event_emitter` now stores a heap emitter in a hidden own property of the public resource object, `__perryAsyncResourceEventEmitter`, and `asyncResource.eventEmitter` reads it back from there. The property is an ordinary traced field, so the collector rewrites it on evacuation and it lives exactly as long as the resource object. An emitter that has become unreachable along with its resource is still collected. A strong root from the immortal backing would have kept every such emitter alive for the life of the process. The key is listed in `is_internal_runtime_key_bytes`, so reflection never reports it. Stdlib and ext emitters are stable numeric handles in the handle band and stay in the backing word. `js_event_emitter_async_resource_subclass_init` now passes the rooted resource, not the pre-allocation raw pointer.

Adds `gc::tests::runtime_roots::hook_dispatch_handles::test_async_resource_event_emitter_link_follows_a_moved_subclass_emitter`. It fails when the heap emitter goes back into the backing word and when the key is dropped from the internal-key list. Also adds `test-files/test_gap_11258_eventemitter_async_resource_subclass_gc.ts`.
