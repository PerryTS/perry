- **fix(gc): the copying minor remembers a rejected raw word the way the coverage walk decodes it (#11353).** `restore_surviving_dirty_coverage` skips each object that the dirty scan reported complete. The justification is that `visit_slot_with_parent` has already re-remembered every slot of that object with the predicate the walk applies. For a raw (untagged) word, the two predicates differed:
  - The visit uses the copier's validating classification, which requires a plausible header at `addr - 8`.
  - The walk uses `decode_heap_addr`, which accepts any address in a registered range.

  A stale raw word that named nursery garbage was therefore not remembered by the visit, yet counted as "needs tracking" by the walk. The unit-test cross-check caught this and panicked with `N page(s) of M dirty-scan-covered object(s) are not remembered`. In the perry-runtime suite the word sat in slot 8 of a dead old array left behind by an earlier test, on a page dirtied during `json_construction_growth_mixed_layouts_and_errors_leave_a_walkable_heap`. The failure depended on address layout: in a loaded mass run it hit 8 of 301 runs on main and 0 of 301 on this branch.

  When the visit rejects a word, the remembering arm now asks `decode_heap_addr`, which is the walk's own question and also the write barrier's. Regression test: `gc::tests::copy_slot_decode::a_raw_nursery_word_the_copier_rejects_is_still_remembered_for_the_coverage_walk`. It plants the address of a young object's zeroed second field in an old parent's slot, and without the fix it fails with the issue's exact message.
