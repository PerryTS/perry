AsyncLocalStorage, AsyncHook, AsyncResource, and domain instances are now ordinary JavaScript objects that own GC-finalized native payloads. This removes their handle-recognition registries and raw backing allocations, and preserves Node-compatible identity, prototypes, reflection, and lifecycle behavior. Disabling storage or hooks releases the native payload immediately; reuse restores the backing while callbacks remain in traced object state. Retired hook delivery records are reused, keeping create/disable churn bounded.
