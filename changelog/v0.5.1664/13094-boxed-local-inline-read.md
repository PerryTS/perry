- **perf(codegen): a boxed local is read inline in its declaring function** (#13094).
A local that a closure captures and the body reassigns (solid's `runTop` walks its captured `node` parameter) was read through the `js_box_get_bits` accessor on every use. The read is now a range test, one header-word compare of the box cell and one load, with the accessor kept on the cold arm for the entry sentinel and TDZ reads, so a TDZ read still throws the named ReferenceError. Witness: a captured, reassigned list-walk parameter uses 22.5% fewer instructions; opencode TUI typing -1.5%. Gap test: `test_gap_boxed_local_read.ts`.
