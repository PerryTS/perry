Read kind and birth relationships directly from shape records. Delete the
separate 128 KiB per-agent kind cache, alternate tag encoding and invalidation.

A prototype's shape carries the weak ShapeId of its ordinary keyless receiver
birth. Every use validates the live birth record's complete facts and reads its
current width and tracking count. Alternating prototypes no longer repeat the
exact-facts search at every birth. Absent or retired relationships resolve
through the existing interner. The scalar fits existing extension padding on
64-bit targets; record/slab geometry and GC carrier lifetimes are unchanged.
No cache, registry, latch, name check or alternative allocator is added.
